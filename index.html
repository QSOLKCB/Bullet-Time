THE FIELD MANUAL

BEND THE SECOND.

Bullet time slows the world to under a quarter speed. Your aim stays at full speed, you move at half speed and your guns cycle almost normally. It drains the hourglass meter; kills refill it, and it slowly recovers to 30% on its own.

Shootdodge: press Shift (or B) while moving to launch into a slow-motion dive in that direction. With no direction you dive backwards. Diving is free, makes you hard to hit and doubles as a way through glass. You land on your back for a moment; you can still shoot from the floor.
